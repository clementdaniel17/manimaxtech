FABRICATED PROTOTYPE
UAV ANTENNA · POLYIMIDE

Flexible dual-band dish-shaped printed antenna

A lightweight polyimide antenna with a dish-shaped copper radiating patch, designed for UAV command-and-control and payload data links. Its flexible substrate is intended to support conformal integration over curved UAV surfaces.

2.435 GHzResonant band 1
4.181 GHzResonant band 2
59.46 × 48.96 mmReported dimensions
1 mmPolyimide thickness
−21.083 dBReturn loss at 2.435 GHz
−12.853 dBReturn loss at 4.181 GHz

Reported VSWR: 1.187 and 1.574. Reported gain: 4.1 dBi and 3 dBi, respectively. The supplied document also reports an estimated antenna weight of 4.13 g on polyimide.

FLEXIBLE · MINIATURISED

Novel high-gain flexible miniaturised antenna

A compact microstrip patch antenna using copper radiating and ground layers on a flexible polyimide substrate. The supplied design description identifies the 5.8 GHz ISM band as the intended operating region for UAV high-definition video links.

GARUDA-INSPIRED RADIATING GEOMETRY
MULTIBAND · FR4

Multiband Garuda logo antenna for UAV applications

A compact planar microstrip antenna fabricated on an FR4 substrate. Its segmented emblem-inspired radiator and slot geometry are designed to produce multiple resonances for UAV telemetry and data connectivity applications.

2.32 GHzBand 1 · S11 −34.577 dB
3.53 GHzBand 2 · S11 −30.44 dB
4.83 GHzBand 3 · S11 −26.95 dB
6.55 GHzBand 4 · S11 −20.05 dB
46.86 × 38.43 mmReported footprint
VSWR 1.038–1.220Across four resonances

The supplied document reports a single-layer structure and describes potential UAV telemetry, sub-6 GHz and payload-data applications.

ANTENNA PROTOTYPE SET
SRR · DEFECTED GROUND

Dual-band miniaturised DGS antenna with embedded SRR

A compact flexible printed antenna using an embedded square split-ring resonator and a defected ground structure on polyimide. The design description identifies UAV command, control, telemetry and high-speed data transmission as target applications.

2.45 GHzBand 1
5.72 GHzBand 2
23.24 × 19.48 mmReported dimensions
−31.60 dBReturn loss at 2.45 GHz
−28.62 dBReturn loss at 5.72 GHz
1.053 / 1.076Reported VSWR

The supplied document reports realised gains of −17.6 dBi and 2.75 dBi at the two bands. These unusual values should be checked against the final measurement/simulation report before public promotion.
