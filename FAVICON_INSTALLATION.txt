MANIMAX TECH FAVICON

The favicon is based on the existing MANIMAX TECH antenna/drone emblem.
It is already linked in all seven HTML pages.

Included files:
- assets/favicon.ico (browser compatibility)
- assets/favicon-16.png and favicon-32.png (browser tabs)
- assets/favicon-512.png (high-resolution icon)
- assets/apple-touch-icon.png (iOS home-screen icon)

No additional installation is required. Upload the complete website folder to your hosting account.
If the old icon remains visible after deployment, hard-refresh the page or clear the browser favicon cache.
